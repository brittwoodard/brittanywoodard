My journey didn't start behind a screen. It started with a degree in Cultural Anthropology and a deep-seated restlessness. While my peers were content in the lecture hall, I felt like I was depreciating, learning about the world instead of experiencing it.

- |

In 2013, my obsession with travel and spirituality finally collided. I began learning and teaching yoga across Southeast Asia, Australia, and Europe. During those years, I discovered something about myself. While I loved the practice, I was captivated by the process. I found more joy in building the sales page for a retreat than I did teaching it. I was a "wellness visionary" who secretly had the heart of a developer.

- |

This curiosity led me to help scale a global online Ayurvedic school from the ground up. As a startup, I was the one behind the scenes, testing every CRM and budget until we found the perfect flow. I realized then that the most soulful businesses often struggle because their "plumbing" doesn't match their purpose.

- |

In 2022, I took the leap into a coding bootcamp to marry my industry intuition with technical precision.

- |

Today, I build websites and systems that look as elevated as the work my clients do. I speak the language of Yoga and Ayurveda, but I build the backend that makes it scalable.
