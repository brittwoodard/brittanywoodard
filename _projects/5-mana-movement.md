  The client’s vision for the future of the Mana Movement brand extends beyond her personal brand, requiring a scalable online academy designed for a three-phase launch. She needed a digital space that felt light, feminine, and accessible, all in one. place. The goal was to build a high-performance system that could grow alongside her vision, ensuring the technology supports, rather than dictates, the community experience.

  To bring this vision to life, I built a custom-coded website integrating her robust CRM. The architecture was strategically mapped to support a three-phase launch, ensuring the platform is accessible while allowing for functionality to be added as the academy grows.
outcome: |
  The new academy provides an easy experience for students. By centralizing the email marketing with the member's portal, we eliminated the manual admin. Mana Movement has a scalable, high-performance digital foundation that embodies the feminine, accessible nature of her movement while supporting its continued growth.
