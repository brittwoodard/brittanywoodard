vision: |
  The client needed a strategic sales page to enroll students into a one-time internship program. The challenge was to build a webpage filled with information that addressed all prospective intern queries, without overwhelming them with too much text. The design needed to capture the unique blend of Indian and Balinese cultures.
strategy: |
  Because this was a one-time offering, I built a high-performance, "no-cost" system rather than relying on a heavy CRM. In addition to designing a sophisticated, highly informative website, I integrated a custom, secure onboarding workflow that legally protected the client and automated the entire process, allowing them to fill the retreat without overhead.
outcome: |
  The result is a stunning website that resonates with the brand’s "India meets Bali" ethos. This system successfully filled the retreat without the overhead of expensive software, providing a journey that drives prospective interns to take the next step on their Ayurvedic path.
