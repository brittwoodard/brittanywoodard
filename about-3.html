In 2013, my curiosity for spirituality and my love for travel finally collided. I began learning and teaching yoga throughout Southeast Asia, Australia, Inida, and Europe. Eventually, I found an online Ayurvedic school (this was pre-COVID, before the world moved online) where I could explore my curiosity for healing while living out of a suitcase. It felt like magic!

But every time I planned a new workshop or retreat, my favorite part wasn't the teaching. It was creating the marketing materials and the systems behind them.

I found myself more excited about building a sales page for a workshop than actually leading it. I was eventually hired at that same Ayurvedic school; it was a startup, which gave me the incredible opportunity to do everything. We tried every CRM and budget under the sun until we found what worked.
